Sound Frequency Generator

Control Explanations

  • Frequency: The pitch of the sound in Hertz (Hz). Higher values mean higher pitch.
  • Waveform: The shape of the sound wave.
    • Sine: Smooth, pure tone.
    • Square: Harsh, buzzy tone.
    • Sawtooth: Sharp, biting tone.
    • Triangle: Mellow, flute-like tone.
  • Volume: The loudness of the track.
  • Pan: Stereo positioning. -1 is full left, 1 is full right, 0 is center.
  • Detune: Fine-tunes the frequency in cents (100 cents = 1 semitone). Creates a chorus effect when used with multiple tracks.
  • Mute: Silences the track without stopping it.
  • Attack: Time taken for the sound to reach full volume after starting.
  • Decay: Time taken for the sound to drop from full volume to the sustain level.
  • Sustain: The volume level held while the sound is playing (after attack and decay).
  • Release: Time taken for the sound to fade out after stopping.
  • Filter Type: Determines which frequencies are allowed to pass through.
    • Lowpass: Allows low frequencies, cuts high ones (muffles sound).
    • Highpass: Allows high frequencies, cuts low ones (thins sound).
    • Bandpass: Allows a specific range of frequencies (nasal sound).
  • Filter Freq: The cutoff frequency for the filter.
  • Filter Q: Resonance or "sharpness" of the filter peak. Higher values emphasize the cutoff frequency.
